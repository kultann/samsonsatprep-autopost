Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Samson SAT Prep publishing tool ("the app"), which publishes SAT study content to the @samsonsatprep Instagram and TikTok accounts.

What the app accesses

The app connects only to accounts owned by Samson SAT Prep. With the account owner's permission it uses basic account information (account ID and username) and permission to publish posts to that account.

What the app does not do

The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal information about other users. It does not read messages, followers' data or comments.

Storage

Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets and used only to publish scheduled posts. They are never shared with third parties.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from Instagram (Settings → Apps and websites) or TikTok (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions).
